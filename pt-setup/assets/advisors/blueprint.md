# Blueprint — $1–10M ARR

## Signals
- $1–10M ARR; first reps hired; the playbook is still being written by the founder.

## Focus first
- Keep the 3x OTE bar: a $250K OTE rep should produce about $750K in new bookings.
- Teach in three steps: Confirm Need → Why the category → Why your product.
- The founder writes the playbook personally; nobody else can yet.
- Professional services can be a wedge if they stay at or under 25–30% of revenue.

## Do not focus on
- Leading with the product instead of the need.
- Adding reps before the ramp model works.

## Benchmarks
- First ramped reps hitting ~3x OTE on the written playbook.

## Exit criteria
- The playbook is standard; new reps hit the ramp without rewriting it.
