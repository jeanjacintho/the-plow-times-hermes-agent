# Discovery — $0–1M ARR

## Signals
- Pre-repeatable revenue; founder still sells; a handful of design partners.

## Focus first
- Talk to customers until one painful need is unmistakable.

## Do not focus on
- Hiring a sales team before you can sell it yourself.
- Building platform features for a market you have not confirmed.

## Benchmarks
- A few design partners who will take the call again.

## Exit criteria
- A need you can repeat in one sentence; first paid logos in sight.
