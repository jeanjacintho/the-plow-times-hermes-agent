# Discovery — $0–1M ARR

## Signals
- The offer has to be a painkiller, not a vitamin: buyers feel the cost of not solving it this quarter.
- Product-market fit here looks like 5–10 customers who will take a reference call.

## Focus first
- Talk to tens or hundreds of customers before you pitch investors.
- Founder-led selling is teaching and insight — you are learning the need, not qualifying people out.
- Keep repeating the painful need in one sentence until someone else can say it back.

## Do not focus on
- Hiring a sales team to find a motion you have not found yourself.
- Pitching VCs before those customer conversations exist.
- Building platform features for a market you have not confirmed.

## Benchmarks
- Design partners who will take the call again beat a polished deck.

## Exit criteria
- You can name the need without slides, and first paid logos are in sight.
